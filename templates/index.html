Why Arbitrum?

🐢

Why L2 is Needed

Ethereum Mainnet (L1) became congested, leading to high gas fees and slow transactions. Layer 2 solutions were created to take computation off-chain while relying on L1 security.

⚙️

Optimistic Rollups

Arbitrum uses Optimistic Rollups to batch thousands of transactions off-chain and submit them to Ethereum as one single transaction. It assumes transactions are valid until proven otherwise.

💸

10x Cheaper Txns

The real-world benefit: Users pay fractions of a cent on gas compared to mainnet. This enables micro-transactions, fast DeFi trading, and high-performance Web3 gaming.
